#### F319 · medium · effort M · ~10 tab/section load failures bypass DetailLoadError: no Retry, and three of them print raw `error [code]: message`
`apps/desktop/web-rs/src/views/enterprise_application_detail_pane/access.rs:190` · confirmed; also reported as F379
**Problem.** frontend-workspace.md calls `DetailLoadError` the universal "message + Retry" block.
```rust
<Body1 class="form-error">
    {format!("error [{}]: {}", e.code, e.message)}
</Body1>
```
The sign_in.rs comment cites a "detail-pane `error [code]: message` convention" that survives only in those three sites. The verifier notes that add_owner.rs and bulk_action_bar.rs are search-result strings rather than tab loads, and that readiness_view.rs already has a retry button.
**Proposal.** Replace each `Err(e) => <Body1 class="form-error">` in a Suspend load path with `<DetailLoadError error=e on_retry=... />`; every tab already owns a `reload` signal. Render sign_in.rs and readiness_view.rs message-first with the code muted, fix the stale comment, and add a repo_invariants scan rejecting `form-error` inside a `Suspend` `Err` arm.

### Performance

Seven of the bucket's fourteen medium findings sit here. They are not hot loops but avoidable network work: bypassed single-flight gates, over-broad cache invalidation, repeated Graph chains, an unretried token endpoint and a cache attached to the wrong read.

#### F007 · medium · effort S · list_applications_with_pairing bypasses the SP-index single-flight gate and has no gate of its own; two frontend surfaces call it
`apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/commands/applications/mod.rs:186` · confirmed
**Problem.** The `None` arm calls the client directly under `try_join`, so `state.single_flight(sp_index_key)` never sees it, and nothing gates `apps_pairing_key` either:
```rust
let (apps, sps) = futures::future::try_join(
    client.list_applications_all(query, Some(APPS_MAX)),
    client.list_service_principals_index(),
```
cache.rs documents that the sibling `indexes_cached` was rewritten to avoid exactly this, and `sp_index_watch` exists only to serve the bypass. On a cold tenant, Home mounting plus a click on App Registrations runs two `/applications` and two `/servicePrincipals` scans. Any concurrent gated reader adds a third SP scan.
**Proposal.** Replace the arm with `try_join(client.list_applications_all(..), cache::sp_index_cached(&state, &client, &tenant_id))`, which keeps concurrency and inherits the gate and the correctly keyed watch. Wrap the miss path in `state.single_flight(&cache_key)` with a post-lock re-check as `search_corpus` does; `sp_index_watch` and the `Some(cached_index)` arm then become unnecessary.
